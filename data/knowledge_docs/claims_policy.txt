VERISK PROPERTY ESTIMATING NETWORK — CLAIMS POLICY GUIDE

CLAIM STATUS DEFINITIONS

OPEN: Claim received and logged in the system. Adjuster assigned. Initial review pending.

IN_REVIEW: Adjuster is actively reviewing the claim. Property inspection may be scheduled. Evidence being gathered.

WORK_ORDER_ISSUED: Adjuster has reviewed the claim and issued a work order to an assigned contractor. Repair work is authorized to begin.

REPAIR_IN_PROGRESS: Contractor has begun repair work on the property. Work order is active.

INVOICE_SUBMITTED: Contractor has submitted an invoice for completed work. Adjuster review of invoice is pending.

CLOSED: Claim fully resolved. All work completed and invoices paid.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Claimants must submit photographic or video evidence of:
- Exterior damage (roof, walls, foundation)
- Interior damage (ceilings, floors, structural elements)
- Personal property damage (if applicable)
- Pre-loss condition documentation (if available)

Evidence must be submitted within 30 days of the loss event.

WORK ORDER PROCESS

1. Adjuster reviews claim and selects a licensed contractor from the Verisk network.
2. Work order is created specifying scope of repairs.
3. Contractor acknowledges and begins work.
4. Contractor provides status updates at minimum weekly.
5. Upon completion, contractor submits invoice for adjuster review.
6. Adjuster approves or disputes invoice within 5 business days.

INVOICE GUIDELINES

Invoices must include:
- Itemized list of all materials
- Labor hours and rates
- Subcontractor costs (if applicable)
- Work order reference number
- Completion date

Invoices submitted without proper documentation will be returned for correction.

DISPUTE RESOLUTION

If a claimant disputes the repair scope or estimate, they may request a second adjuster review within 15 days of work order issuance. All disputes are escalated to a Senior Adjuster.
