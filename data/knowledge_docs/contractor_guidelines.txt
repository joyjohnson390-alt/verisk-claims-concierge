VERISK PROPERTY ESTIMATING NETWORK — CONTRACTOR GUIDELINES

NETWORK REQUIREMENTS

All contractors in the Verisk PEN must maintain:
- Active state contractor license (verified against license database)
- General liability insurance minimum $1M per occurrence
- Workers' compensation coverage for all employees
- Verisk contractor certification (renewed annually)

WORK ORDER ACCEPTANCE

Upon receiving a work order assignment:
1. Acknowledge the work order within 24 hours.
2. Contact the claimant within 48 hours to schedule initial site visit.
3. Confirm site visit date and time with adjuster via the portal.

STATUS UPDATE REQUIREMENTS

Contractors must submit status updates:
- Within 24 hours of beginning work
- Weekly throughout the repair period
- Immediately upon encountering scope changes or unexpected damage

SCOPE CHANGES

If you discover damage beyond the original work order scope:
1. Stop non-emergency work on the expanded scope.
2. Document with photos and written description.
3. Submit a scope change request through the portal.
4. Do NOT proceed with additional repairs until adjuster approves the scope change.

Unauthorized work outside the approved scope will not be reimbursed.

INVOICE SUBMISSION

Invoices must be submitted within 10 business days of work completion.
Required documentation:
- Signed completion certificate from claimant
- Itemized material receipts
- Labor log with hours and rates
- Before/after photographs

ACCULYNX INTEGRATION

Estimates created in AccuLynx are automatically synced to the Verisk PEN within 4 hours. Contractors using AccuLynx for estimates do not need to re-enter data in the portal — the systems are integrated.
